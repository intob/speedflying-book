Chamonix is one of my favourite areas for speedflying. It is where I began flying, after a couple of flights in the UK. Living in Chamonix, I was able to exceed 1000 flights per year very easily.

The valley tends to be very flyable.

# Summer season

## Le Tour
Le Tour lift will likely open around 8th June, this makes the hike for Glacier du Tour line very easy (1h rather than 2h).

Speed-soaring is possible at Le Tour, and I do it a lot. Lamina valley breeze of 45kmh from July, peaking around 64kmh mean for 1/2 weeks in August. The lift normally closes around 8th September.

## Brevent & Plan Praz
Brevent & Plan Praz will likely open around 15th June. This is good for laps, although not the most beautiful. You can also hike from Brevent summit to access other lines.

## Plan de L'Aiguille
There are some really good lines from Plan de L’Aiguille that are closed during July and August. Normally you can do 6 per day there, but sometimes there is a queue for the lift.
